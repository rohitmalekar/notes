---
title: Ethereum & Institutional Finance
description: Essays on how banks, market infrastructure and regulators could adopt Ethereum for tokenisation, settlement and verification, and what stands in the way.
---

India has already decided to tokenise, and Singapore and Hong Kong are running pilots. These essays look at what that infrastructure should run on: privacy, settlement, governance, and why technical merit alone rarely decides adoption. Weekly coverage of regulation and pilots across Asian finance lives at [The Monsoon Ledger](https://themonsoonledger.com/); the long-form research is [Ethereum and Indian Institutional Finance](https://institutions.ethindia.co/briefing.html) for ETHIndia.

- [[Being Right Isn't Enough - Lessons for Ethereum from a 90s OS War]]: what a forgotten OS war teaches about why superior technology loses
- [[The Decentralized Web and the Rise of Transparency-First Institutions]]: what changes when institutions operate on public, verifiable infrastructure
