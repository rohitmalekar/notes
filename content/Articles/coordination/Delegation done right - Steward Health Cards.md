#Web3 

*Last Updated: May, 2023*

Gitcoin implemented Steward Health Cards in collaboration with Karma to address the challenge of assessing the level of engagement and activity of stewards in the Gitcoin DAO. The Health Cards use metrics such as Snapshot voting and Forum activity to quantify steward activity, with different weights assigned to different types of contributions. The algorithm calculates a Health Score for each steward, which is updated daily and displayed on a custom dashboard. This initiative aims to improve transparency, accountability, and the ability for token holders to delegate their tokens to the most active stewards. The delegation portal further enhances transparency by providing information on steward contributions and allows stewards to create profiles, link social handles, and accept delegations. These tools contribute to the decentralized and transparent nature of DAO governance and help ensure active participation and accountability among stewards in the Gitcoin community.
