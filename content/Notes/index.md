This is my thinking in public, before it becomes essays. Each note captures one idea, kept deliberately small so it can link to others. The connections matter more than any single note. The library grows a little every week.

Two collections:

- **[[Notes/self/index|Reflections]]** — first-person syntheses: observations and arguments I'm working out in my own words.
- **[[Notes/reference/index|Reference Notes]]** — ideas from books, papers, and talks, each with its source cited.
