> **Title:** Overcoming Social Differences for Coordination
> **Tags:** Decentralization

> **Reference:** Elinor Ostrom

 
The problem is less coordinating public goods per se but rather one of helping communities made up of imperfectly cooperative but socially connected individuals overcome their social differences to coordinate at scale in broader networks.
