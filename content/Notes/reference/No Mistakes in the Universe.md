 **_“There are no mistakes in the universe”_**

 There are only stubborn repeat reminders that the universe will keep doing onto you until you ignore what you are doing to yourself.
 
 Everything that happens to you is an opportunity to make a choice. Choices lead to actions, repeated actions become habits. Habits shape character and character drives [[Building Destiny|destiny]] (from Brihadaranyaka Upanishad IV.4.5)
