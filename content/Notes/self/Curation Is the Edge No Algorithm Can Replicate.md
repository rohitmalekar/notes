title: Curation Is the Edge No Algorithm Can Replicate
tags:

---

A personal repository of tested insights, refined over time, becomes a competitive edge that no algorithm can replicate. Not because the information is secret—most of it isn't. But because the curation is yours. What you chose to capture, how you connected it, and which frameworks survived contact with reality. This is the difference between information and knowledge: one is abundant, the other is earned. The machine can access everything; it can't tell you what mattered.
