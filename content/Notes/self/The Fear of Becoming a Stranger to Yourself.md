title: The Fear of Becoming a Stranger to Yourself
tags:
- Well-Being
type: Reflection
date: 2024-05-10
---

While we change jobs and relationships, hoping for better outcomes, we are stuck with the same problems in a loop because we rarely change ourselves. Even with an understanding of who we need to become, the fear of becoming a new version of ourselves—a stranger—can be daunting.
