> [!info] Note metadata
> **Title:** Definition of Product
> **Tags:** Product
> **Type:** Reflection

Product: An artifact that meets the stated and unstated needs of a user through the harmonious interplay of passive experience (e.g. a movie) or active interactions (e.g. a football) or both (e.g. a car).
