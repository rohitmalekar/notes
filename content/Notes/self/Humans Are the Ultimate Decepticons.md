We commit choices tainted with biases. We chase desires we don't fully understand. Our thoughts are laced with flawed perspectives. We handle the uncomfortable in our life by numbing whatever little attention is left in life by devouring endless entertainment.

But what really makes us the apex decepticon is the underlying irony. The deceiver and the deceived are one and the same soul.

If we were created by design and not accident, someone is having a great laugh out there.

May be that's what Krishna referred to as the लीला (lila), the Creator's cosmic joke, where consciousness hides behind illusion only to amuse itself in the grandest play of all.
