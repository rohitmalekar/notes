> [!info] Note metadata
> **Title:** Impact of Life Lessons on Identity
> **Tags:** Well-Being
> **Type:** Reflection

Life lessons emerge from a deep-rooted underlying shortcoming. Such situations often require extended experiential learning from mentors that lasts years. If done right, life lessons create such a paradigm shift that the learnings become an integral part of your identity.
