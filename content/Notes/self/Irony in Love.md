Is there a greater irony than how the very flaws that blind two souls into believing they are meant to be together [[No Mistakes in the Universe|are the same flaws]] that [[We Are Prisoners of Our Own Biases|make it impossible]] for them to stay together? Falling in love is easy the part. Rising in one is a different drama altogether.
