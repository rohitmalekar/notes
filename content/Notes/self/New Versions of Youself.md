> **Title:** New Versions of Youself
> **Tags:** Well-Being
> **Type:** Reflection

The first time you stay out of touch from your closest friend, the first time you go back to your hometown to see the street you grew up on all changed, the first time you witness age in your parents is the time you realise life runs version control on you. And that the cozy comfortable version you are living in the present has an expiration date and when that life is overriden, so do you, with a new version of yourself.
