> [!info] Note metadata
> **Title:** High Quality Problem
> **Tags:** Leadership, Well-Being
> **Type:** Reflection

 
 A high-quality problem enables you to **self-discover a first principle that brings a material shift** in how you perceive the world around you. 

The pursuit for a high-quality problem is intrinsic motivation, creates space to **embrace your vulnerabilities,** and expands your worldview.
