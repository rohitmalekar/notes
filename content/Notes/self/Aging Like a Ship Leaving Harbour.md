title: Aging Like a Ship Leaving Harbour
tags:
- Well-Being
type: Reflection
date: 2026-05-08
---

Aging is so much like a ship leaving the harbour. Slow but certain. Things that were once close, drift away. Sights that were certain, get hazy on the horizon. And eventually, you can no longer see them.

Just like the things that once were second nature, that you can no longer do. Well, at least not without wrecking something else in your body.
