Most of the self-help + influencer industry related to acquiring new habits and skills in India, whether it's personal finance or health, feels like vapourware. Everyone attempts to inspire or intimidate you to change how you think without first changing the person you are. Eventually, people lose themselves to the natural state of their mind and their money to workshops and courses.
