> [!info] Note metadata
> **Title:** Being Authentic in Interviews
> **Tags:** recruiting
> **Type:** Reflection

It might feel counter-intuitive, but if you stretch too much to pretend who you aren't during a job interview, then at best, you won't get the role. At worst, you might and you will struggle for months to be someone who you are not with your future teammates.
