Have you ever held a thought and not believed in it?

Let a belief sit still long enough for questions to surface. Been the toughest critic of yourself, not from self-doubt but from that quiet certainty that some thoughts your mind makes up are just stories.

Something shifts when you stop treating setbacks as mistakes, knowing they are just stubborn repeat reminders that the universe will keep doing onto you until you ignore what you are doing to yourself in those stories.

That's a freedom worth chasing, the freedom from your own mind.
