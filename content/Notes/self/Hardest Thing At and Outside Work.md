The hardest thing you will ever work on in your career will likely not be even remotely close to the hardest thing you need to work on in your personal life, at least for the vast majority of us.

Corollary: A vast majority of us will go to the world’s end to find the next hardest thing to crack in our career than face what we need to have a go at in our personal life.
