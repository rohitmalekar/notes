Nothing beats the mental gymnastics to justify falling for a temptation, except, the self-deceit to rationalize and live with the consequences after.

If science ever advances far enough to discover the vocabulary to measure each species by their adherence to their dharma, dogs will probably top the list and we, masters at both forms of the art, will decorate the bottom.
