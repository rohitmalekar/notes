> **Title:** DAOs as a Social Tech
> **Tags:** Web3, DAO
> **Type:** Reflection

DAOs are a social technology, barring a few outliers. Doubling down only on the "code is law" ethic without providing necessary rituals in the social layer to build legitimacy is a critical design flaw.
