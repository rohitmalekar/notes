title: We Don't Have One Life
tags:

type: Reflection
date: 2025-08-12

We live many lives, each bounded by events that change us, from one chapter of life to the next. And when enough pages fill up, try peeking at your version from an earlier time. I bet, you will feel like staring at a stranger and so will the future you, when they recall the chapter you are writing right now.
