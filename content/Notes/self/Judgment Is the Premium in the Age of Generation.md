---
title: Judgment Is the Premium in the Age of Generation
tags:
  - AI
  - Leadership
type: reflection
date: 2026-01-14
---

When anyone can generate plausible-sounding content in seconds, the premium shifts to judgment. To taste. To knowing *which* ideas matter and *why*. AI can summarise a hundred articles, but it can't tell you which ones changed how you see the world. It can generate frameworks, but it can't know which framework actually worked when you tried it in your own context. That kind of knowledge that is experiential, contextual, and hard-won lives only in you. And it stays with you only if you've built a place for it.
