> **Title:** Parents can Enable the Freedom to Discover
> **Tags:** Parenthood

People with close relationships with their parents: What have they done right?

Freedom of space and choice to explore, tumble, and discover our own world views rather than imposing theirs from their battle scars. And once you have kids of your own, you realize it is so darn hard to pull off.
