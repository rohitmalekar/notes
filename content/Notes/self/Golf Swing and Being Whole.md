> **Title:** Golf Swing and Being Whole
> **Tags:** Well-Being

The golf swing that feels the best is the one in which you are whole with the experience - fluid and effortless; the ball just happens to be in its path. Quite like life itself, [[Irony in Finding Purpose|when you are whole with what's around you]], achieving goals just happens to be a byproduct.
