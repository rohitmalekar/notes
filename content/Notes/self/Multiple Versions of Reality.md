**Composed reality:** I need to sort out what's on my plate before Monday kicks in.

**Perceived reality:** I am floating on a molten rock tossed around in eternal space at unimaginable speeds.

**Absolute reality:** We do not have the powers in our faculties, the precision in our instruments, vocabulary in our languages, or access to the dimensions required to know what's really real. 🤷‍♂️
