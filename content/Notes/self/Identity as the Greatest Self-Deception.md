---
title: Identity as the Greatest Self-Deception
tags:
- Well-Being
type: Reflection
---

Yes, it is your body.

But the body is not you.

And neither are its desires, emotions, feelings, perceptions, biases, anguishes, pleasures, needs and aspirations.

Yes, you need to take care of the house you live in. That doesn't make you the house, does it?

There is no [[Neti Neti|greater self-deception]] known to mankind than believing otherwise.
