> [!info] Note metadata
> **Title:** Fallibility of Thoughts
> **Tags:** Well-Being
> **Type:** Reflection

Thoughts and the resulting words, at best, are a lossy compression of incomplete perspectives dented by unconscious biases built over unreliable memory and informed by faulty faculties. Best served after sleeping over charged topics.
