> **Title:** Untied by Duality of Good and Bad
> **Tags:** Leadership

The biggest green flag for me in someone as a leader is when they don't believe life can fit within oversimplified lenses of good and bad constructed by socially accepted subjective realities. They would defer their judgement until eternity if they could.
