tags:

My most bullish population-scale use case for AI is that more individuals shed their hard-wired siloed work identities as engineer or designer or marketer, weaken their attachment to specific tools or frameworks, and unlock their genuine care and commitment to solve real world problems using the AI-enabled affordances.
