> **Title:** Product Managers and NFL
> **Tags:** Product, Careers
> **Type:** Reflection

When solving for "are we building the right product?", PMs are like the running back, partnering to discover a clear path to the goal. When solving for "are we building the product right?", PMs are like the offensive linemen, protecting and blocking what might impede progress.
