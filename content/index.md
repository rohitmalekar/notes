## About
I work at the intersection of data, funding systems, and coordination for digital public goods. I design grant programs, build analytics tools, and help complex ecosystems with better decision-making, so communities and funders understand what matters, where capital flows, and how to support the projects that create real public value.

My path here wasn't linear. Over 18 years across consulting, product, and research roles, I moved from structured environments focused on strategy and analytics toward open ecosystems where incentives, governance, and impact are harder to model but more meaningful to shape.

## Let's Collaborate
I work with teams and funders who are building public-interest technology and need clearer strategy, better data, or stronger funding systems.

If you're designing a grant program, evaluating impact, or trying to make sense of a complex ecosystem, I can help.

**Ways I typically support teams**  
- Designing and operating grant or funding programs  
- Building analytics dashboards and decision-support tools  
- Ecosystem research, retrospectives, and sensemaking reports

- Workshops and advisory support for alignment and coordination

Open to short-term consulting, fractional roles, or project-based collaborations.
