## About
I write **[The Monsoon Ledger](https://themonsoonledger.com/)**, a weekly on policy, regulation and Ethereum across Asian finance, and was lead researcher on **[Ethereum and Indian Institutional Finance](https://institutions.ethindia.co/briefing.html)** for ETHIndia. My focus is tokenisation, settlement and verification in India, Singapore and Hong Kong.

I spent twenty years inside large institutions and four years inside Ethereum. My work now sits where the two meet. Strategy and analytics roles at Deloitte and Medallia taught me how institutions evaluate and adopt technology. Allocating and measuring capital onchain at Gitcoin, Metagov and Scroll taught me how Ethereum behaves in production. [[about|More about me →]]

## 🏦 Current Work
- **[The Monsoon Ledger](https://themonsoonledger.com/)**: weekly briefings for banks, market infrastructure and regulators across India, Singapore and Hong Kong. Regulatory Wire tracks circulars as they land, Pilot Scorecard follows institutional pilots, and Under the Hood explains the systems behind them. • [Subscribe](https://themonsoonledger.com/)
- **[Ethereum and Indian Institutional Finance](https://institutions.ethindia.co/briefing.html)** (ETHIndia, 2026): lead researcher on a seven-module briefing that starts from India's tokenisation mandates under SEBI and RBI and asks what they should run on. Every figure is source-linked in a public ledger with verification tiers. • [Briefing](https://institutions.ethindia.co/briefing.html)
