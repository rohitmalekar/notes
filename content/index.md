## About
I work at the intersection of data, funding systems, and coordination for open source ecosystems. I've co-led grants rounds at Gitcoin, built analytics dashboards used in live funding decisions across the Ethereum ecosystem, and contributed governance research to ENS DAO through Metagov.

My path here wasn't linear. Twenty years across consulting, product, and research, including strategy and analytics roles at Deloitte and Medallia, gave me the institutional rigor I now bring to coordination systems. [[about|More about me →]]

***Recent contributions at:** [Gitcoin](http://gitcoin.co) · [Open Source Observer](http://opensource.observer) · [Metagov](https://metagov.org) · [Scroll](https://scroll.io)* 

## 🛠️ Recent Work
- **ENS DAO Governance Research Platform** — Built the analytics for Metagov's independent governance retrospective of ENS DAO; supported a reproducible data stack (Dagster + dbt + DuckDB) across 7 onchain and forum data sources, now powering a live research platform cited in active governance decisions over a $400M+ treasury. • [Dashboard](https://ensretro.metagov.org) • [Repo](https://github.com/metagov/ENS-Retro-Data) • [Report](https://discuss.ens.domains/t/ens-retro-draft-final-report/22067)
- **GG24 Capital Allocation Analysis** — Demonstrated that Quadratic Funding reliably funds visible, legible work but structurally underfunds ecosystem-critical infrastructure, a finding that directly informs how domain operators design future rounds • [Analysis](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/mapping-problems-to-capital-insights-from-gitcoin-grants-24/24962) • [Dashboard](https://gg24-analysis.fly.dev)
- **GG24 Interop Standards, Infrastructure & Analytics Round** — Co-designed and operated Ethereum's inaugural interop-focused grants domain; a $100K+ QF round on Giveth to fund the open standards, tooling, and measurement systems underlying Ethereum's multi-chain future • [Domain Design](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/domain-announcement-interop-standards-infrastructure-analytics/24631) • [Interop Landscape](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/gg24-update-inside-the-interop-standards-infra-analytics-round/24749) • [Retrospective](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/gg24-interop-round-retrospective/24936)
- **Scroll Delegate Accelerator: Metrics Module** — Designed and delivered a data-driven governance curriculum to 30 delegate candidates, equipping them to evaluate Scroll governance proposals with onchain metrics • [Curriculum](https://scrollzkp.notion.site/6-A-useful-approach-to-metrics-2ac7792d22af80f488cce5b6e7dfd508)
