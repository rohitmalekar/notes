Cycling is probably the only other thing I can manage at a stretch for twice as many hours longer than I sleep. Bangalore doesn't make it easy to pedal within the city, but here are some longish paths I have found worth meandering around the town.

If you need a handy checklist for long distance riding, check it out [[BRM Checklist|here]].

- A 100 km [round trip to Thalli](https://www.strava.com/activities/17584905579), the erstwhile Little England (March 2026)
- A 300 km [Bangalore - Mysore - Bangalore](https://www.strava.com/activities/16630944357/overview) bag-packing ride (December 2025)
- Incomplete [Rajyotsava 200K BRM](https://www.strava.com/activities/16323228895) but a memorable 185 km ride (November 2025)
- [Village 200K BRM](https://www.strava.com/activities/15797152375) attempt paused by puncture (September 2025)
- An Imperial Century (160 km) [ride to Chintamani](https://www.strava.com/activities/15715781660) (September 2025)
- A 100 km downhill and back uphill from [Bangalore to Shoolagiri](https://www.strava.com/activities/15625655302) (August 2025)
- Century Ride to [Dodda Maralwadi](https://www.strava.com/activities/15406839835) and back via Jigni (August 2025)
- A one-way [115 km ride](https://www.strava.com/activities/14721947150) through North Cauvery Wildlife Reserve to [Anchetty](https://x.com/RohitMalekar/status/1931331549460602898) (June 2025)
- To [Hesaraghatta Lake](https://x.com/RohitMalekar/status/1920721817016431059) and back via ORR for a [115 km ride](https://www.strava.com/activities/14402871792) (May 2025)
- A [100 km loop](https://www.strava.com/activities/13829982479) through [farmlands around Bannerghatta National Park](https://x.com/RohitMalekar/status/1898663007489495241) (March 2025)
- A [scenic route](https://x.com/RohitMalekar/status/1872135084373155862) looping [200 km](https://www.strava.com/activities/13182702803) in North of Bangalore (December 2024)
- [Highlights](https://x.com/RohitMalekar/status/1858011114845442400) from [80 km ride](https://www.strava.com/activities/12911194973) around Khao Yai National Park outside Bangkok (November 2024)
- A [100 km](https://www.strava.com/activities/12151805274) route around [ORR including Avalahalli Forest](https://x.com/RohitMalekar/status/1824014720694710637) in Bangalore (August 2024)
- [Hennur Bamboo Forest and East Bangalore](https://x.com/RohitMalekar/status/1776542220923134213) stomping for a [100 km](https://www.strava.com/activities/11116808756) loop (April 2024)
- A [165 km loop](https://www.strava.com/activities/10456363780) between the [city and the airport](https://x.com/RohitMalekar/status/1740770660862095396) (December 2023)
- Banks of Cauvery River in [Bheemeshwari](https://x.com/RohitMalekar/status/1721147159834505456) ([85 km each way](https://www.strava.com/activities/10164240034)) (November 2023)
- [100 km loop](https://www.strava.com/activities/9242862059) through cityscapes and farmlands to Chikkatirupati and back (June 2023)
- Westbound to the [beautiful Big Banyan Tree](https://x.com/RohitMalekar/status/1474575631597785088) for a [90 km ride](https://www.strava.com/activities/6424537128) (December 2021)
